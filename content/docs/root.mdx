  a Root and reads from it.

## API reference

Give it `iiifContent`: a Manifest, a Collection, a Canvas or a content state, as a URL or
an object, in Presentation 2, 3 or 4. The parts inside it show that resource and move
through it together: choose a thumbnail in `Items`, and the `Body`, the `Label` of the
Canvas and the `Panel` follow. It renders a `div` of your own layout, and announces each
move to assistive technology.

Its most used props are `iiifContent`, `canvasId` with `onCanvasChange` (the current Canvas,
controlled), `language`, `request` and `highContrast`. Every prop is in
[Composing layouts](/docs/composing#root), with how to lay its components out. The Viewer and
its templates are each a Root and its parts, and take the same props.

## A Root inside a Root

A Root can hold another, as a context of its own. Each part reads its nearest parent
Root, as a web component's element reads its nearest parent `clover-root`: the inner Root
loads its own resource and keeps its own place in it, and announces its own moves.
Nothing passes between them. Choosing a thumbnail in the inner Root's `Items` moves the
inner Root alone, and its `request` and `language` are its own. Use it to show a second
resource in the same layout: a related Manifest, say, beside the one the page is about.

Two things are the page's, not either Root's. The page's language is read once, at the
outer Root. And the higher-contrast mode of the outer Root holds for everything inside
it, the inner Root too. Full screen takes the Root its control is in, the inner one for
the inner Root's parts.

```jsx
<Clover.Root iiifContent={url}>
  <Clover.Label as="h3" />
  <Clover.Body />
  <aside>
    <Clover.Root iiifContent={related}>
      <Clover.Label as="h4" />
      <Clover.Items />
    </Clover.Root>
  </aside>
</Clover.Root>
```

```html twin
<clover-root
  iiif-content="https://clover.iiif.library.northwestern.edu/mocks/paged/manifest.json"
>
  <clover-label as="h3"></clover-label>
  <clover-body></clover-body>
  <aside>
    <clover-root iiif-content="https://example.org/related/manifest.json">
      <clover-label as="h4"></clover-label>
      <clover-items></clover-items>
    </clover-root>
  </aside>
</clover-root>
```
