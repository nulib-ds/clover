## API reference

Opened on a Manifest, its title and summary head the grid, a cell for each view (a
spread is one cell). Opened on a Collection, the Collection's own title and summary head
it, a cell for each member; choosing one shows that Manifest in the lightbox. Opened on
an AnnotationPage of tags across Manifests, its label heads the grid, a cell for each
tag, its region cut from its view; choosing one shows its Manifest in the lightbox, framed
on the tag, and the lightbox's strip steps through the tags. The grid is inside the
gallery's inset, its cells the inset apart.

The lightbox is a layer over the gallery, filling its bounds: modal within it, the rest of
the gallery out of reach while it is open. Its title is the Manifest's label, with Back
before it, a step back out to the grid, named for where it goes (the Collection, or the
annotations' page, where the gallery opened on one), and Close after it. Under its view, the view's own label,
then a short strip of thumbnails that moves on, focus on the view shown, so the arrow keys go on at
once; or, with `lightboxItems="pager"`, Previous and Next, focus on Close. Beside its view is the information panel, as Default's and Document's is beside theirs: docked where there is room, its toggle on the bar, and sliding over the view where there is not. Escape, Close or
a press outside its panel closes it too, and focus goes back to the cell. Its Body loads
as the pointer or focus first reaches the gallery.

<ApiReference elements="clover-viewer">

| Prop               | Type                               | Default                     | Description                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `items`            | `"grid" \| "masonry"`              | `"grid"`                    | The grid, as its Items' layout: square cells, or each picture at its own proportions, in reading order.                                                                             |
| `secondary`        | `"summary" \| "navDate" \| "none"` | `"summary"`                 | The line under each cell's label, where it has one (a Collection's members do): its summary, its date, or none.                                                                     |
| `summary`          | `boolean`                          | `true`                      | The resource's summary under the title, cut to two lines with Read more; `false` leaves it out.                                                                                     |
| `size`             | `string`                           | `10rem`                     | A cell's width (sets `--clover-items-size`).                                                                                                                                        |
| `gap`              | `number \| string`                 | The inset                   | The space between cells: pixels, or a CSS length.                                                                                                                                   |
| `height`           | `"auto" \| number \| string`       | `"auto"`                    | `auto` grows with the grid; a height (pixels, or a CSS length) holds it, the grid scrolling under the header.                                                                       |
| `lightboxItems`    | `"slider" \| "pager"`              | `"slider"`                  | How the lightbox moves on from its view: a short strip of thumbnails, or Previous, the position and Next.                                                                           |
| `panel`            | `boolean`                          | `true`                      | The information panel beside the lightbox's view, and its toggle, as Default's and Document's.                                                                                      |
| `defaultPanelOpen` | `boolean`                          | `true`                      | Whether the panel starts open beside the view; over it, closed.                                                                                                                     |
| `panelLayout`      | `"accordion" \| "tabs"`            | `"accordion"`               | The panel's sections as stacked boxes, or as tabs.                                                                                                                                  |
| `panelSections`    | `PanelSection[]`                   | All                         | Which of the panel's sections it may have; each still shows only where the resource has data for it, and no panel at all where it has none of them.                                 |
| `panelLabels`      | `PanelSectionLabels`               | —                           | What a panel section says, in place of Clover's own word for it: text, or text by language tag.                                                                                     |
| `download`         | `boolean`                          | `true`                      | The downloads in the panel's About, where there is a `rendering`.                                                                                                                   |
| `languages`        | `boolean`                          | `true`                      | The languages to read the resource in, beside the panel's About heading, where it comes in several.                                                                                 |
| `storageKey`       | `string \| false`                  | `"clover-template-gallery"` | Where the panel's width is remembered; `false` forgets it.                                                                                                                          |
| `headingLevel`     | `number`                           | `2`                         | The title's level; the lightbox's title is one below.                                                                                                                               |
| `collapseControls` | `"auto" \| "always"`               | `"auto"`                    | When the lightbox's view controls (zoom, rotation, reset, full screen) collapse behind one button: only where its row cannot hold them all, or always, leaving the media more room. |
| `canvasOptions`    | `CloverCanvasOptions`              | —                           | The lightbox's Canvas `options`: its gestures, zoom limits and [motion](/docs/canvas#motion).                                                                                       |

</ApiReference>

The lightbox fills the gallery's bounds by default. Give it room, a backdrop and corners
with three properties, set on the gallery or any element around it:
`--clover-template-lightbox-inset` (default `0`), `--clover-template-lightbox-backdrop`
(`transparent`) and `--clover-template-lightbox-radius` (`0`).

## Examples

### Masonry

Use `items="masonry"` for pictures at their own proportions, each packed up under the one
above it, still read left to right.

<ViewerExample resource="catalog" template="Gallery" props={{ items: "masonry" }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Gallery} items="masonry" />
```

</ViewerExample>

### A Collection

Opened on a Collection, the gallery is the Collection's: its title and summary, and a cell
for each member, its summary under its label.

<ViewerExample resource="collection" template="Gallery">

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={collection} template={Gallery} />
```

</ViewerExample>

### Size

Use `size` to set the width of the cells.

<ViewerExample resource="catalog" template="Gallery" props={{ size: "8rem" }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Gallery} size="8rem" />
```

</ViewerExample>

### The lightbox's pager

Use `lightboxItems="pager"` for Previous, the position and Next under the lightbox's view,
in place of its strip of thumbnails.

<ViewerExample
  resource="catalog"
  template="Gallery"
  props={{ lightboxItems: "pager" }}
>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Gallery} lightboxItems="pager" />
```

</ViewerExample>

### A height of its own

Use `height` to hold the gallery to a height: its header stays, and the grid scrolls under
it. The lightbox fills that height.

<ViewerExample resource="catalog" template="Gallery" props={{ height: "28rem" }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Gallery} height="28rem" />
```

</ViewerExample>

### Gap

Use `gap` to set the space between cells, the gallery's inset unless set.

<ViewerExample resource="catalog" template="Gallery" props={{ gap: "0.5rem" }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Gallery} gap="0.5rem" />
```

</ViewerExample>

### The lightbox with a backdrop

Give the lightbox room around its panel and a backdrop, so the gallery shows dimmed
around it.

<ViewerExample
  resource="catalog"
  template="Gallery"
  vars={{
    "--clover-template-lightbox-inset": "var(--clover-inset)",
    "--clover-template-lightbox-backdrop": "rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)",
    "--clover-template-lightbox-radius": "var(--clover-radius)",
  }}
>

```jsx
<Viewer
  iiifContent={url}
  template={Gallery}
  style={{
    "--clover-template-lightbox-inset": "var(--clover-inset)",
    "--clover-template-lightbox-backdrop": "rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)",
    "--clover-template-lightbox-radius": "var(--clover-radius)",
  }}
/>
```

</ViewerExample>
