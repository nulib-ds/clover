## API reference

The toolbar holds the title, and at its end the provider's logo, then a globe for the
languages where the resource comes in several, a button for each section the resource has,
Download where there is a `rendering`, and Search. A section's button opens it alone in an
aside beside the pages, with its own bar, where there is room for both, and over them
where there is not.

The pages run down one column, a spread to a sheet, the top of the next always in sight:
they scroll freely, as a PDF reader's continuous view does, and PageUp and PageDown go a
sheet at a time. Tab reaches the page in view, and from there Up and Down step to the
previous page and the next, Home and End to the first and last, and Enter opens it. A press on a page, the pointer a magnifier over it, or a zoom on its
Canvas opens it as the full viewer, the Canvas's own controls at its top end, and over
its top left an arrow back to the pages, then Previous and Next; the arrow, or Escape,
closes it. A video or a sound waits as its still on black, with a play button over it, until it
is pressed into. A resource of one page has its thumbnail too, put away to start, unless
`singleItem={false}` leaves it out.

The thumbnails' toggle sits on the bar between them and the pages; drag the bar, or use
the arrow keys on it, to widen or narrow them. On a phone the thumbnails float over the
page, and choosing one puts them away. Size it with `--clover-template-document-height`.

<ApiReference elements="clover-viewer">

| Prop                 | Type                    | Default                      | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `defaultSidebarOpen` | `boolean`               | Several pages                | Whether the page thumbnails start shown: by default where the resource has several pages, a single page's put away; over the page, not.                                                                             |
| `singleItem`         | `boolean`               | `true`                       | The page thumbnails and their toggle where the resource has one page too.                                                                                                                                           |
| `defaultPanelOpen`   | `boolean`               | `false`                      | Whether the aside starts open on About beside the page; over it, closed.                                                                                                                                            |
| `panelLayout`        | `"accordion" \| "tabs"` | `"accordion"`                | Not read by Document: each section opens alone in its aside.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `panelSections`      | `PanelSection[]`        | All                          | Which sections the toolbar offers; each still only where the resource has data for it.                                                                                                                              |
| `panelLabels`        | `PanelSectionLabels`    | —                            | What a section's button and the aside call it, in place of its word in Clover's interface language: text, or text by language tag.                                                                                  |
| `download`           | `boolean`               | `true`                       | The Download button, its links in a menu under it, where there is a `rendering`.                                                                                                                                    |
| `languages`          | `boolean`               | `true`                       | A globe among the toolbar's controls, where the resource comes in several languages, that sets the one it reads in; `false` leaves it out.                                                                          |
| `interfaceLanguages` | `boolean`               | `true`                       | The globe also offers Clover's interface languages (its control labels, section titles and announcements) that the resource has none of, the visitor's and your site's, where they differ; `false` leaves them out. |
| `headingLevel`       | `number`                | `2`                          | The title's level; the aside's name a level below, and its section's headings a level below that.                                                                                                                   |
| `storageKey`         | `string \| false`       | `"clover-template-document"` | Where the bars' widths are remembered; `false` forgets them.                                                                                                                                                        |
| `collapseControls`   | `"auto" \| "always"`    | `"auto"`                     | For the Body's own controls (Choice, a Canvas's information, and in the full viewer the view's): only where their row cannot hold them all, or always.                                                              |
| `canvasOptions`      | `CloverCanvasOptions`   | —                            | The Body's Canvas `options`: its [gestures, zoom limits and motion](/docs/canvas#interaction).                                                                                                                      |

</ApiReference>

## Examples

### Thumbnails put away

Use `defaultSidebarOpen={false}` to start with the page thumbnails put away, their toggle
over the page's top left.

<ViewerExample
  resource="catalog"
  template="Document"
  props={{ defaultSidebarOpen: false }}
>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Document} defaultSidebarOpen={false} />
```

</ViewerExample>

### Aside open

Use `defaultPanelOpen` to start with the aside open on About beside the pages. On a
phone, where it would cover them, it starts closed whatever you pass.

<ViewerExample
  resource="catalog"
  template="Document"
  props={{ defaultPanelOpen: true }}
>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Document} defaultPanelOpen />
```

</ViewerExample>

### One page

A resource of one page keeps its thumbnail and the thumbnails' toggle. Use
`singleItem={false}` to leave them out.

<ViewerExample resource="photo" template="Document" props={{ singleItem: false }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Document} singleItem={false} />
```

</ViewerExample>
