## API reference

The Viewer draws Default when it is given no `template`. Over the Body are the title and
the provider's logo; opened on a Collection, the Collection's title is under the
Manifest's, with a pager among its members. Under the Body, the thumbnails, headed by the
titles of the current view and where it is among them. A Manifest of one view
has no thumbnails unless `singleItem` asks for them.

The information panel is beside all three, the layout's full height, and a bar between
shares the width: drag it, or use the arrow keys on it. Where there is no room for both,
on a phone, the panel slides over the Body instead, and a press beside it, Escape or its
close button puts it away. Size the Body with `--clover-body-height`.

<ApiReference elements="clover-viewer">

| Prop               | Type                                    | Default                     | Description                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `header`           | `boolean`                               | `true`                      | The title and provider over the Body; opened on a Collection, the Collection's title over the Manifest's and a pager among its members; for a content state's several targets, a pager among them. |
| `headingLevel`     | `number`                                | `2`                         | The title's level; the panel's sections are a level below.                                                                                                                                         |
| `panel`            | `boolean`                               | `true`                      | The information panel and its toggle.                                                                                                                                                              |
| `download`         | `boolean`                               | `true`                      | The downloads in the panel's About, where there is a `rendering`.                                                                                                                                  |
| `languages`        | `boolean`                               | `true`                      | The languages to read the resource in, beside the panel's About heading, where it comes in several; `false` leaves the choice out.                                                                 |
| `defaultPanelOpen` | `boolean`                               | `true`                      | Whether the panel starts open beside the Body; over it, closed.                                                                                                                                    |
| `panelLayout`      | `"accordion" \| "tabs"`                 | `"accordion"`               | The panel's sections as stacked boxes, or as tabs.                                                                                                                                                 |
| `panelSections`    | `PanelSection[]`                        | All                         | Which of the panel's sections it may have; each still shows only where the resource has data for it, and no panel at all where it has none of them.                                                |
| `panelLabels`      | `PanelSectionLabels`                    | —                           | What a panel section says, in place of Clover's own word for it: text, or text by language tag.                                                                                                    |
| `items`            | `"slider" \| "list" \| "grid" \| false` | `"slider"`                  | The thumbnails under the Body, or none.                                                                                                                                                            |
| `singleItem`       | `boolean`                               | `false`                     | The thumbnails even where the resource has one view.                                                                                                                                               |
| `storageKey`       | `string \| false`                       | `"clover-template-default"` | Where the panel's width is remembered; `false` forgets it.                                                                                                                                         |
| `fill`             | `boolean`                               | `false`                     | Fill the box it sits in; the Body takes what the rest leave.                                                                                                                                       |
| `collapseControls` | `"auto" \| "always"`                    | `"auto"`                    | When the Body's view controls (zoom, rotation, reset, full screen) collapse behind one button: only where its row cannot hold them all, or always, leaving the media more room.                    |
| `canvasOptions`    | `CloverCanvasOptions`                   | —                           | The Body's Canvas `options`: its [gestures, zoom limits and motion](/docs/canvas#interaction).                                                                                                     |

</ApiReference>

## Examples

### Thumbnails

Use `items` to lay the thumbnails under the Body out as a slider, a list or a grid, or
`false` to leave them out.

<OptionsExample
  prop="items"
  label="items"
  options={["slider", "list", "grid", false]}
  resource="catalog"
>
<div data-option="slider">

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} items="slider" />
```

</div>
<div data-option="list">

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} items="list" />
```

</div>
<div data-option="grid">

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} items="grid" />
```

</div>
<div data-option="false">

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} items={false} />
```

</div>
</OptionsExample>

### One view

Use `singleItem` to show the thumbnails even where the resource has one view, which has
nothing to choose between.

<ViewerExample resource="photo" props={{ singleItem: true }}>

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} singleItem />
```

</ViewerExample>

### Header and panel

Use `header={false}` to leave out the title over the Body, and `panel={false}` to leave
out the information panel and its toggle. `defaultPanelOpen` starts the panel open or
closed beside the Body. On a phone, where it would cover the Body, it starts closed
whatever you pass, and its toggle opens it.

<ViewerExample resource="film" props={{ header: false, panel: false }}>

```jsx
<Viewer
  iiifContent="https://api.dc.library.northwestern.edu/api/v2/works/d8fb6329-f7fb-48bf-8bb7-70a64ded5a15?as=iiif"
  header={false}
  panel={false}
/>
```

</ViewerExample>

### Fill

Use `fill` to have the Viewer fill a box of a set height, the window under a page's own
header, say. The Body takes what the title and the thumbnails leave, never less than
16rem; a shorter box scrolls.

<ViewerExample resource="postcard" box="34rem">

```jsx
<div style={{ height: "34rem" }}>
  <Viewer
    iiifContent="https://api.dc.library.northwestern.edu/api/v2/works/0e66d6d8-5dc5-4686-b056-d18682c79ebf?as=iiif"
    fill
  />
</div>
```

</ViewerExample>
