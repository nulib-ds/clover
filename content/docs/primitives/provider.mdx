## API reference

Reads `provider` from the Root's resource and renders a `ul`, an item for each provider:
its [logo](/docs/primitives/logo) and its name, linked to its first homepage. A provider
with no name of its own is named by its homepage. It renders nothing while there is none,
and its other props go to the `ul`. A provider whose logo fails to load keeps its name,
linked; one that drew its logo alone is left out, so no empty link is left behind.

`Provider` is a compound part: what it holds is drawn once for each provider, inside its
link, and the parts in it read that provider rather than the resource. `Logo` draws its
logo, `Label` its name, and `Homepage`, `SeeAlso` and `Id` its own links. A part of your
own reads it with `Clover.useProvider()`. The Panel's About section shows it whole, and
the Default and Document templates its logo alone, at the end of their title's line.

<ApiReference elements="clover-provider">

| Prop        | Type                              | Default               | Description                                                       |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `of`        | `"manifest" \| "canvas"`          | `"manifest"`          | Whose `provider`: the resource's, or the Canvas the reader is on. |
| `value`     | `{ id, label, homepage, logo }[]` | —                     | Providers to show instead of the Root's, as IIIF gives them.      |
| `link`      | `boolean`                         | `true`                | Link each provider to its homepage, when it has one.              |
| `children`  | `ReactNode`                       | its logo and its name | What to draw for each provider.                                   |
| `className` | `string`                          | —                     | Added to `clover-provider`.                                       |

</ApiReference>

## Examples

### Its logo alone

Hold a `Logo`, and each provider is its logo alone, linked to its homepage and named by
the provider for assistive technology, as the templates show it.

<Example>
<PartDemo part="Provider" holds={[{ part: "Logo" }]} />

```jsx {2-4}
<Clover.Root iiifContent={url}>
  <Clover.Provider>
    <Clover.Logo />
  </Clover.Provider>
</Clover.Root>
```

</Example>
