# Cookbook Recipes

<Cookbook4Demo>

The [IIIF Cookbook](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/) is a set of recipes showing how to model real-world resources in IIIF. Below is every recipe it advertises, grouped as on iiif.io. Choose **View** to open one in Clover's [Viewer](/docs/viewer), which slides in over the page. Clover does not handle every recipe yet. To open a resource of your own, give its address to the [homepage](/)'s field.
