## API reference

Plays the Presentation 4 Timeline you hand it, or loads one itself (`iiifContent`: a
Manifest gives its first Timeline). Inside a `Root`, given nothing, it plays the Root's
view when that is a Timeline; [`Body`](/docs/body) draws a Timeline view with it. A
Presentation 3 Canvas of sound alone is a Timeline once upgraded, so the Viewer plays one
with it too. It fills its parent, so give the parent a height.

It plays through [Canvas](/docs/canvas), with its transport, captions, chapters and
annotations on the recording's time, and shows the Timeline's accompanying or placeholder
Canvas while it plays.

| `timeline`        | `object`                                             | The Root's view             | A Presentation 4 Timeline to play; `toPresentation4()` upgrades older ones. |

| `label`           | language map or `string`                             | The Timeline's label        | The view's accessible name.                                                 |
| `language`        | `string`                                             | The reader's, or the Root's | The language to read labels, captions' names and annotations in.            |
| `controls`        | `boolean \| { annotations, information }`            | `true`                      | The round controls over the Body; the transport stays.                      |
| `placeholder`     | `boolean`                                            | `true`                      | Open on the Timeline's `placeholderContainer`, where it has one.            |
| `annotations`     | `{ annotation, targetIndex, pageLabel?, pageId? }[]` | The Root's                  | Annotations on its time, each a cue while its stretch plays.                |
| `chapters`        | `{ id?, label, lang?, start, end? }[]`               | The Ranges, in a `Root`     | Chapters, on its seek bar and in a menu.                                    |
| `caption`         | `string \| null`                                     | —                           | The caption file (`id`) to show while captions are on.                      |
| `onCaptionChange` | `(caption: { id, language? } \| null) => void`       | —                           | The reader chose a caption file, or Off (`null`).                           |
| `onMediaElement`  | `(element: HTMLVideoElement \| null) => void`        | —                           | The recording's element, for a transcript of your own.                      |
| `onEnded`         | `() => void`                                         | —                           | Playback reached the end: a cue to move on.                                 |
| `options`         | `object`                                             | —                           | Canvas's: `request` and `hideDelay` among them.                             |
| `className`       | `string`                                             | —                           | Added to `clover-canvas clover-timeline`, on its `figure`.                  |

## Formats

Timeline plays what a browser's audio and video elements play: MP4 (AAC), MP3, Ogg and
WebM, and HLS streams through hls.js, which loads only for one. The `format` a recording
declares is passed to the browser as it is. A body of Presentation 4's type `Audio` plays,
and so does Presentation 3's `Sound`.

The IIIF Cookbook's recipes for sound play with it:
[0002](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0002-mvm-audio/) in either version, 0014's
accompanying image, 0103's annotations and 0434's choice of formats.

## Accessibility

Timeline's transport is the one Canvas plays video with. Every button and slider is
named and has its key in its popover, and the controls stay on screen while keyboard
focus is in them. With the view focused, Space or K plays and pauses, J and L skip ten
seconds back or forward, and M mutes.

Captions come from the Timeline's WebVTT files, each in its own language, and its
plain-text transcript shows in the Panel's Transcript. A recording never starts by
itself: it plays when the reader presses Play, or follows a link or the Manifest's
`auto-advance`.
