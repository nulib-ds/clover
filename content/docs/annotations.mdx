# Annotations

What the annotations of the Root's resource say (comments, tags, transcriptions) by set,
as a list to read and to point with. Pressing one finds it on the Canvas; an eye before
each set hides its annotations there. The `Panel`'s Annotations section is this part,
listing every Canvas's. In a layout of your own, place it anywhere inside a `Clover.Root`.

```jsx
import * as Clover from "@nulib/clover";
import "@nulib/clover/styles.css";

<Clover.Root iiifContent={manifestUrl}>
  <Clover.Content />
  <Clover.Annotations />
</Clover.Root>;
```

## What it lists

By default, every annotation in the Manifest, as the `Panel` lists them (see
[Every Canvas's annotations](#every-canvass-annotations)); `scope="view"` lists the
view's alone, the Canvases on show, a page or a spread. They come from each Canvas's `annotations`, the AnnotationPages fetched as the reader reaches
them, and from the pages the Root's `annotations` prop adds, after the Manifest's own.
Painting annotations are the picture, not commentary, and caption files belong to the
[Transcript](/docs/transcript), so neither is listed. A Content Search's hits are drawn
on the Canvas, and listed by [Search](/docs/search), not here.

Each AnnotationPage is a set, headed by an eye that hides it and, when the page has a
`label`, by that label as a heading. Each annotation is an entry in the set's list:

- **What it says.** Text bodies by their `format`: plain text, Markdown, or IIIF's limited
  HTML, sanitized. Image bodies as images
  ([Cookbook 0377](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0377-image-in-annotation/)).
- **What it marks.** A small picture of its region of the Canvas, cut by the image's
  server (a level 1 or 2 service; none for a level 0 or a plain image). On a recording,
  the time it starts.
- **Where it leads.** A `linking` annotation
  ([Cookbook 0022](https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0022-linking-with-a-hotspot/))
  adds "Open the link": a web page opens in a new tab; a Canvas or a Manifest is shown in
  the Root, and focus moves to the Canvas, since the list may change under the button.

With no annotations in view, it renders nothing.

## Finding one on the Canvas

Pressing an entry anywhere frames its annotation on the Canvas, going to its Canvas first
if need be. For the keyboard, each entry holds a button that does the same, out of sight,
its focus drawn round the entry. The button is named "Find on the image" and the entry's
text, or "Find in the recording" for one on a recording's time. It is `aria-pressed`
while its annotation is the one picked. An entry with no text is named by its number in
its list, as its hotspot is, so no two read alike.

Hovering or focusing an entry highlights its hotspot, and hovering a hotspot highlights
its entry: one highlight, shared through the Root, with no timers. The pick is shared
too: one picked on the Canvas, or in its annotations menu, is picked here. An annotation
on a recording's time is found by seeking there; see
[Annotations on a recording's time](/docs/canvas#annotations-on-a-recordings-time).

## Hiding a set

The eye before a set's label is a checkbox, checked while the set is hidden, the eye
struck through. Its popover says "Hide"; its name is "Hide" and the page's label, or
"Hide Annotations" for a page without one, so no two read alike beside several sets.

Hiding a set takes its annotations off the Canvas only. The list keeps them, to show them
again, and the Canvas's annotations menu lists them still, after the same eye: the hidden
sets are the Root's, shared by both. An annotation of a hidden set, picked, is still
framed.

## Every Canvas's annotations

By default (`scope="all"`) it lists every annotation in the Manifest, as the `Panel`
does, wherever it is placed: a part beside the stage lists what the panel would. While
it shows, every Canvas's annotation pages are fetched, not only the view's;
`scope="view"` lists and fetches the view's alone.

It lists them by layer: AnnotationPages that share a label are one layer, with one eye
that hides them all, however many pages it was published in, and the pages with no label
are another. Within a layer, the annotations are by Canvas, in the Manifest's order, each
Canvas's under its label. A Manifest of one Canvas is listed by layer alone.

The Canvases in view are `aria-current`, their labels marked by the accent's edge at
their start and the stronger text, so by more than color. As the reader moves, their
annotations come to the top of the list's scrolling box, and one picked on the Canvas is
brought into view. Neither happens while the pointer is over the list or focus is in it,
so nothing moves under the reader, and the page itself is never scrolled.

Annotations given as the content, an AnnotationPage or AnnotationCollection made apart
from any Manifest, may be on Canvases of other Manifests. Each of those Canvases is named
by its Manifest's label (and its own, where the Manifest has several), each Manifest
fetched once, when the list first shows.

## Headings

`headingLevel` (default 3) is the level of each set's title, and, listing every Canvas's,
of each layer's. A layer's Canvas labels sit a level below its title, or at its level in a
layer with none. Headings in an annotation's Markdown sit a level below the heading over
them, or at its level where there is none. Set it one below the heading the list sits
under. In a `Panel` of your own, see
[Sections of your own](/docs/panel#sections-of-your-own).

A set with a title is a `section` named by it, and a Canvas's group in a layer is one
named by its label.

## Languages

Each set's title, Canvas label and annotation carries its own `lang`, and text in `none`
takes the page's. Clover's own words ("Find on the image") carry theirs where it differs
from the page's. Where either is not in the page's language, an entry's button is named
by its content, each part marked with its own (WCAG 3.1.2).

An image with no text beside it is described by its own label, else its annotation's,
else "Image". Beside text, it says nothing more (`alt=""`), since the text describes it.

## Your own rendering

`renderEntry` draws an entry's content in place of Clover's. The entry keeps its region's
picture, its link, its button and its highlight.

```jsx
// Each text body as plain text, in its own language.
function Entry({ entry }) {
  return entry.bodies
    .filter((body) => body.value)
    .map((body, index) => (
      <p key={index} lang={body.language}>
        {body.value}
      </p>
    ));
}

const renderEntry = (entry) => <Entry entry={entry} />;

<Clover.Annotations renderEntry={renderEntry} />;
```

An entry (`AnnotationEntry`) has its `id`, its set's `setId`, the `canvasId` it targets,
its parsed `target`, its `motivation`, its read `bodies`, and the `annotation` as
published.

## Props

| Prop           | Type                    | Description                                          |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| `scope`        | `"all" \| "view"`       | The Manifest's annotations (default), or the view's. |
| `renderEntry`  | `(entry) => ReactNode`  | Your own rendering of an annotation's content.       |
| `headingLevel` | `2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | Default `3`. See [Headings](#headings).              |
| `as`           | element type            | Default `div`.                                       |

Its other props (`id`, `className`, `style`, `aria-*`) go to its element, and so does its
`ref`.

### Styles

`.clover-annotations` is the list's root, with `data-scope="all"` for every Canvas's. A
set is `.clover-annotations-set`, its header `.clover-annotations-set-header` (the eye is
`.clover-hide`) and its title `.clover-annotations-title`. A Canvas's group is
`.clover-annotations-canvas`, under `.clover-annotations-canvas-label`. Each entry is
`.clover-annotations-entry`, with `data-selected` and `data-highlighted`; inside it,
`.clover-annotations-thumbnail` or `.clover-annotations-time`, then
`.clover-annotations-body`, holding `.clover-annotations-text`,
`.clover-annotations-image` and `.clover-annotations-link`.

A reset that takes the markers off every list takes them off a Markdown body's too; see
[Styles](/docs/composing#styles) to put them back.

## From Clover 3.x

| 3.x `Viewer`                                          | Clover 4                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `informationPanel.renderAnnotation: false`            | The `Panel`'s `Tab`s, without Annotations                                  |
| `informationPanel.annotationTabLabel`                 | A `Tab` with your `label` round `<Clover.Annotations />`                   |
| `informationPanel.defaultTab: "manifest-annotations"` | `<Clover.Panel defaultValue="annotations" />`                              |
| Captions in the Annotations tab                       | The [Transcript](/docs/transcript); `Annotations` leaves caption files out |
| `options.annotations.motivations`                     | Not in Clover 4: every annotation is listed but painting ones and captions |
