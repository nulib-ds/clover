## API reference

Inside a `Root`, it lists every Canvas's annotations, and those of pages the Root's
`annotations` adds, by layer (AnnotationPages that share a label) and then by Canvas; with
none, it renders nothing. Pressing one finds it on the Canvas, and shows its layer again if it was hidden; the
eye at the end of a layer's heading hides the layer there, and lights the entries it
hides while it is pointed at or focused. A still image's OCR (`supplementing` text on its lines or words)
is neither listed nor drawn: a [search](/docs/search) finds in it and draws its hits. A
transcription of the whole page, captions and a recording's text show as ever. In a box
that scrolls, the Canvases in view come to its top as the
reader moves. Where a layer gives the same words as separate annotations, one per language,
it lists one language at a time, offered by their codes in its header: the Root's
`language` to start, else the reader's. Its other props, and its `ref`, go to its element.

<ApiReference elements="clover-annotations">

| Prop           | Type                                    | Default | Description                                                         |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `scope`        | `"view" \| "all"`                       | `"all"` | Every Canvas's annotations, all fetched as it shows, or the view's. |
| `renderEntry`  | `(entry: AnnotationEntry) => ReactNode` | —       | Your own rendering of an entry's content.                           |
| `headingLevel` | `number`                                | `3`     | Its first headings: one below the heading over it.                  |
| `as`           | element type                            | `"div"` | The element to render.                                              |
| `className`    | `string`                                | —       | Added to `clover-annotations`.                                      |

</ApiReference>

## Examples

On a recording, each annotation shows when it starts, and pressing it goes there. These
follow the defendants through the film, in two layers; its captions are the
[Transcript](/docs/transcript)'s.

### In several languages

Where a page gives its words once per language, a transcription and its translation, each
an annotation of its own, Annotations offers their languages by code and lists one at a
time. It starts on the Root's `language`, here Arabic, and Arabic text is written right to
left. This manuscript's English is Ariela Marcus-Sells's translation.

### Pages of your own

Give the Root `annotations`, an AnnotationPage or AnnotationCollection of your own, and
its annotations are listed with the Manifest's.

```jsx
<Clover.Root iiifContent={url} annotations={annotationPageUrl}>
  <Clover.Body />
  <Clover.Annotations />
</Clover.Root>
```

```html twin
<clover-root
  iiif-content="https://clover.iiif.library.northwestern.edu/mocks/paged/manifest.json"
  annotations="https://example.org/annotation-page.json"
>
  <clover-body></clover-body>
  <clover-annotations></clover-annotations>
</clover-root>
```

### Your own rendering

Use `renderEntry` to draw an entry's content yourself, from its read `bodies` or the
`annotation` as published. The entry keeps its picture, its link, its button and its
highlight.

```jsx
const renderEntry = (entry) =>
  entry.bodies
    .filter((body) => body.value)
    .map((body, index) => (
      <p key={index} lang={body.language}>
        {body.value}
      </p>
    ));

<Clover.Annotations renderEntry={renderEntry} />;
```

```html twin
<clover-annotations></clover-annotations>

<script type="module">
  document.querySelector("clover-annotations").renderEntry = (entry) => {
    const bodies = document.createDocumentFragment();
    for (const body of entry.bodies.filter((each) => each.value)) {
      const paragraph = document.createElement("p");
      if (body.language) paragraph.lang = body.language;
      paragraph.textContent = body.value;
      bodies.append(paragraph);
    }
    return bodies;
  };
</script>
```
