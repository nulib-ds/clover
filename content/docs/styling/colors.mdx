# Colors

## Contrast guide

Pick a palette, change any color, and copy the CSS the guide writes. Text requires 4.5:1
(WCAG 1.4.3), and so do the round controls' glyphs, since Exit full screen's label shares
their colors. Rings and the marks of a state require 3:1 (1.4.11).

<ContrastGuide />

## Setting colors

Each color is a custom property named `--clover-color-` plus its name in the guide.
[Variables](/docs/styling/variables#colors) lists each with what it colors.

```css
.my-layout {
  --clover-color-accent: #4e2a84;
  --clover-color-secondary: #fafafa;
}
```

The round controls keep their dark fill (`control`) and white glyphs (`control-glyph`) in
a dark mode, since they sit over the picture.

The media player's colors are
[`--clover-player-*`](/docs/styling/variables#the-media-player), and the Canvas's
background is [`--clover-canvas-background`](/docs/styling/variables#the-body).

## High contrast

An accent too light to carry white text at 4.5:1, such as an orange, a yellow or a light
blue, can still be yours. Set `highContrast` on the `Viewer`, the `Root`, or a component
that stands alone:

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} highContrast />
```

The accent then goes only where nothing is read on it: rings, outlines, the line under an
open heading, the marks of a state. Where it would fill behind a glyph or words, the round
controls' dark fill stands in: `control-glyph` on `control`. Under the pointer, the accent
rings the control at half strength, 1px clear, as Items rings an item, and in full once
it's chosen (the language read, a menu open). Focus keeps its own ring. Text in the
accent is your [`accent-text`](/docs/styling/variables#colors) if you set one, else the
text's own color.

Clover turns high contrast on wherever the system asks for more contrast
(`prefers-contrast: more`), whatever your page sets.

## Do

- Set [`secondary`](/docs/styling/variables#colors) to your page's background: most
  components paint no ground of their own, so Clover's text sits on your page.
- Size your page in `rem` or `%`, never pixels on its root, so Clover
  [follows the browser's text size](/docs/styling/fonts#your-pages-size).
- Give a dark mode a lighter `accent-text`, and keep `accent` for rings and fills.
- When you [restyle a state](/docs/styling/classes#classes-and-states), keep a second
  sign beside the accent, as Clover does: a bold label, an underline, a second ring.
- Measure what your page shows, and never round a ratio up: 4.49:1 fails.

## Don't

- Don't mark a state with the accent alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
- Don't swap `control` and `control-glyph` for a dark mode.
- Don't let a reset take the markers off Clover's lists, or
  [put them back](/docs/styling/classes#lists-in-markdown-text).
- Don't bring back [the Body's motion](/docs/styling/variables#the-body) where the system
  asks for reduced motion.
- Don't set a `--clover-*` property that [Variables](/docs/styling/variables) doesn't
  list: Clover sets those itself.
