## Your page's size

Clover sizes its text, spaces and controls on a
[golden scale](/docs/styling/spacing#the-scale) from your page's `1rem`. That's the root's
font size, 16px unless your page or the browser sets another. Change it, and Clover scales
with it:

```css
html {
  font-size: 112.5%;
}
```

## Text sizes

Clover's text inherits your page's font size, or takes one of four sizes a little above
or below your `1rem`. Each is a
[custom property](/docs/styling/variables#sizes-and-spaces) you can set. Change your
`1rem` in the table: the text sizes follow, and so does the Viewer above it.

## Headings

Every title in Clover is a heading at its `headingLevel`, with the
[class](/docs/styling/classes#classes-and-states) `clover-title`, such as a template's
title or the resource's label in About. Its size is your page's for that level. Clover
gives it only a regular weight, no margin and lines a little closer than running text's,
and lets a long word wrap, all with no specificity, so your rule for that level, or for
`.clover-title`, wins:

```css
.clover-title:is(h2, h3) {
  font-size: 1.25rem;
}
```

Set [`--clover-title-weight`](/docs/styling/variables#templates) to change every title's
weight. Clover styles the Panel's headings itself: each section's, and those of the groups
in it, such as Search's pages.
