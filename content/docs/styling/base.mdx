## Composed layouts

A layout you compose takes the same stylesheets, component by component. On `base.css`
alone, each keeps its structure and nothing more: Items' thumbnails touch, and Metadata's
entries run together. `spacing.css` puts the room back between them, and `styles.css` the
rest of the look, such as the pictures' corners and the labels' weight. The title is your
page's heading in every case.

import "@nulib/clover/base.css";

## Using base.css

For a look of your own, use `base.css` in place of Clover's styles. It keeps the structure
of every component and template (what shows, where each box sits, what lies over what,
how large each box and target is, and the cursor over what you can press or drag) and
defines the scale's [custom properties](/docs/styling/variables#sizes-and-spaces). Add
`spacing.css` after it to keep [Clover's spacing](/docs/styling/spacing), or write your
own.

<div data-code-flavor="react">

Import them in place of `styles.css` and `templates.css`, then your own CSS. They serve
the Viewer in every template.

</div>

<div data-code-flavor="web-components">

Link them before the elements' script, then your own CSS. The elements find `base.css` as
they load and bring none of Clover's styles. If it comes later, they keep Clover's rather
than restyle what's already on the page. With a bundler, import
`@nulib/clover/base.css` before `@nulib/clover/web-components`.

</div>

### A layout that works

`base.css` alone gives you a layout that works:

- Whatever lies over other content (a control's name, a menu, a sticky heading, the Panel
  over a narrow page) gets a surface in your page's colors,
  <span data-code-flavor="both">`Canvas` and `CanvasText`</span>, until your
  look replaces it.
- A control Clover's look restyles, such as a language's code or the eye that hides a set,
  stays the browser's radio or checkbox, focus ring and all.
- Margins and paddings that hold the layout stay: an `auto` margin that aligns a box, the
  room PanelToggle keeps in its corner, and Items' zero margins, which undo the 40px a
  browser puts on each side of a figure.
- A hotspot over the picture stays see-through.
- SplitHandle, the bar you drag between the panes to resize the Panel, shows from top to
  bottom in your text's color.

Your look decides colors and focus. Check its colors in the
[contrast guide](/docs/styling/colors#contrast-guide), keep a focus ring on every
control, and keep every target at least 24px.
