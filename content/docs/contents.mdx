# Contents

<Callout type="info">
  `Contents` is not `Content`. [Content](/docs/content) draws the Root's current
  view.
</Callout>

The Manifest's table of contents, from its Ranges (`structures`): the chapters of a book,
the acts of an opera, nested as they nest. The entry the reader is in is marked, and
choosing one goes there. The `Panel`'s Contents section is this part. In a layout of your
own, place it anywhere inside a `Clover.Root`.

```jsx
import * as Clover from "@nulib/clover";
import "@nulib/clover/styles.css";

<Clover.Root iiifContent={manifestUrl}>
  <Clover.Content />
  <Clover.Contents />
</Clover.Root>;
```

## The entries

Each Range is an entry, by its `label` in its own language (or its `id`, with none), and
the Ranges inside it a list under it. A single Range at the top that holds the others is
unwrapped, so its Ranges are the entries. A Range whose `behavior` is `no-nav` is left
out, the Ranges inside it kept. With no Ranges, it renders nothing.

Choosing an entry goes to the first Canvas its Range names, or its first Range's: framed
to its region, where the Range names one, or at its time on a recording. On the Canvas
already playing, it seeks there. A Range that names no Canvas is a disabled entry.

The entry the reader is in is `aria-current`: the deepest whose Range covers the current
Canvas and, on a recording, whose time holds the moment playing. It follows playback and
seeking. It is marked by the accent's edge at its start, a faint tint of the accent and
bolder text, so by more than color; in forced colors, by the edge in the highlight
color.

The entries are buttons in nested ordered lists, in a `nav` named "Contents", in the
reader's language. A page with two gives each its own name, with `aria-label` or
`aria-labelledby`.

## With the seek bar

On a video or sound, the Canvas reads the same Ranges: one whose items name the Canvas
playing with a time (`#t=` or a `FragmentSelector`) is a chapter, on its seek bar and in
its chapters menu. `Contents` and the seek bar share one highlight, through the Root.
Hovering or focusing an entry draws its chapter out on the seek bar, and the chapter under
the pointer on the seek bar marks its entry (`data-highlighted`).

## Props

| Prop | Type         | Description    |
| ---- | ------------ | -------------- |
| `as` | element type | Default `nav`. |

Its other props (`id`, `className`, `style`, `aria-*`) go to its element, and so does its
`ref`. An `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` replaces its name.

### Styles

`.clover-contents` is its root; each list is `.clover-contents-list`, each entry
`.clover-contents-item`, and its button `.clover-contents-button`, with `aria-current`
and `data-highlighted`. A nested list steps in behind a hairline. The entries are rows as
the `Panel`'s other lists are: Annotations, Search's hits, the Transcript's cues.

### Your own

`tableOfContents(manifest)` gives the entries as `ContentsNode`s (`id`, `label`, the
`canvasId` it goes to, every Canvas it covers in `canvasIds`, its `time` or `region`, and
its `children`). `currentContents(nodes, canvasId, time)` gives the id of the entry the
reader is in, and `chaptersOf(manifest, canvasId, duration)` a recording's chapters. All
three are exported from `@nulib/clover`.

## From Clover 3.x

| 3.x `Viewer`                                       | Clover 4                                                          |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `informationPanel.renderContents: false`           | The `Panel`'s `Tab`s, without Contents                            |
| `informationPanel.defaultTab: "manifest-contents"` | `<Clover.Panel defaultValue="contents" />`                        |
| A Range chosen opens its first Canvas              | The same, framed to its region or at its time, where it names one |
