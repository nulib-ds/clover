## API reference

Inside a `Root`, it lists the Manifest's Ranges (`structures`), nested as they nest, in a
`nav` named "Contents": choosing an entry goes to its Canvas, framed to its region or at
its time, and the entry the reader is in is `aria-current`. An entry with entries under
it opens and closes them by the button at the end of its row; they start closed, but for
the entries holding the one the reader is in, open from the top down to it and set in
bold, so the way to it shows. An entry in parts, columns or pages out of order, steps
through them in its Range's order while it is the current one, and the regions any Range
names are drawn on the Canvas as annotations are. The entry the reader is in shows its
Range's `thumbnail` under its words, as a chapter's shows over the seek bar and under it
in the chapters' menu. It renders nothing without Ranges. Its other props, and its `ref`, go to its element. For the view itself, see
[`Body`](/docs/body).

<ApiReference elements="clover-contents">

| Prop         | Type         | Default      | Description                                       |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| `as`         | element type | `"nav"`      | The element to render.                            |
| `aria-label` | `string`     | `"Contents"` | Its name. Give each its own where a page has two. |
| `className`  | `string`     | —            | Added to `clover-contents`.                       |

</ApiReference>

## Examples

### Chapters

On a video or sound, Ranges that name a time are chapters, on the seek bar too. The entry
follows playback, and pointing at either marks the other.

<Example>
<PartDemo part="Contents" resource="footage" body />

```jsx {3}
<Clover.Root iiifContent="https://clover.iiif.library.northwestern.edu/mocks/rudolf-hess-nuremberg/manifest.json">
  <Clover.Body />
  <Clover.Contents />
</Clover.Root>
```

</Example>

### Articles in parts

A Range may read parts of pages, in an order of its own: a newspaper's article in columns,
continued pages later. Its regions are drawn on the Canvas, and while it is the current
entry it steps through them, each framed and marked. This article starts in two columns
on page 2 and goes on in two on page 5.

<Example>
<PartDemo part="Contents" resource="articles" body />

```jsx {3}
<Clover.Root iiifContent="https://iiif.io/api/cookbook/recipe/0025-newspaper-article-index/manifest.json">
  <Clover.Body />
  <Clover.Contents />
</Clover.Root>
```

</Example>

### Without regions

The regions are drawn as the annotation set `CONTENTS_SET_ID`. Hide it, and Contents still
steps through them.

```jsx
clover.setSetVisible(Clover.CONTENTS_SET_ID, false);
```

```html twin
<script type="module">
  // The Ranges' regions are the set "contents".
  document.querySelector("clover-root").clover.setSetVisible("contents", false);
</script>
```

### Your own

Use `tableOfContents` for the same entries, in a table of your own: each has its `label`,
the `canvasId` it goes to, its `time` or `region`, its `parts` where it has some, and its
`children`. `currentContents`
names the entry the reader is in, and `chaptersOf` gives a recording's chapters.

```jsx
const manifest = Clover.useManifest();
const entries = Clover.tableOfContents(manifest);
```

```html twin
<script type="module">
  const root = document.querySelector("clover-root");
  root.addEventListener("clover-ready", () => {
    root.clover.subscribe(() => {
      const entries = root.clover.contents();
    });
  });
</script>
```
