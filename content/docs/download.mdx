## API Reference

Inside a `Root`, it lists the `rendering` of the Canvases in view, under "This page", and
of the Manifest, under "All pages", as links behind one button. With neither, it renders
nothing. Its other props go to the `div` around the button and its list; it takes no `as`
or `ref`.

| Prop           | Type                    | Default                  | Description                                             |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| `children`     | `ReactNode`             | `"Download"`, translated | What the button says.                                   |
| `headingLevel` | `2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | `3`                      | The groups' headings: one below the heading above them. |
| `className`    | `string`                | —                        | Added to `clover-download`.                             |

## Examples

### Heading level

Use `headingLevel` for its groups' headings, "This page" and "All pages": the level below
the heading it sits under, here `4` under an `h3`.

<Example>
<PartDemo
  part="Download"
  resource="page-transcript"
  content
  props={{ headingLevel: 4 }}
/>

```jsx
<Clover.Download headingLevel={4} />
```

</Example>

### Button text

Give the button words of your own as its children, with a `lang` where they are not in
the page's language.

<Example>
<PartDemo
  part="Download"
  resource="page-transcript"
  content
  props={{ children: "Download the text", headingLevel: 4 }}
/>

```jsx
<Clover.Download>Download the text</Clover.Download>
```

</Example>

### In the templates

`Document` puts it in its toolbar, and `download={false}` leaves it out. `Default` lists
the same links in its Panel's About, with no button.

<ViewerExample
  resource="https://nulib-ds.github.io/clover/mocks/page-transcript/manifest.json"
  template="Document"
>

```jsx
<Viewer
  iiifContent="https://nulib-ds.github.io/clover/mocks/page-transcript/manifest.json"
  template={Document}
/>
```

</ViewerExample>

### Without the button

For one resource's `rendering` as a plain list of links, use
[`Rendering`](/docs/primitives/rendering).

<Example>
<PartDemo
  part="Rendering"
  resource="page-transcript"
  content
  props={{ of: "canvas" }}
/>

```jsx
<Clover.Rendering of="canvas" />
```

</Example>
