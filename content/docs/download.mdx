# Download

What the Root's resource offers to download: the `rendering` of the Canvases in view
("This page") and of the Manifest ("All pages"), such as a PDF, a text or a full-size
image, as links behind one button. The `Document` template puts it in its toolbar;
`Default` lists the same links in the [Panel](/docs/panel)'s About instead. In a layout
of your own, place it anywhere inside a `Clover.Root`.

```jsx
import * as Clover from "@nulib/clover";
import "@nulib/clover/styles.css";

<Clover.Root iiifContent={manifestUrl}>
  <Clover.Content />
  <Clover.Download />
</Clover.Root>;
```

It renders nothing when neither the Canvases in view nor the Manifest has a `rendering`.

The links are in two groups, each shown only when it has one: "This page", the
`rendering` of the Canvases in view (both pages of a spread), and "All pages", the
Manifest's. Each group is a `section` named by its heading, at `headingLevel`. A
rendering is listed once in its group. A link reads the rendering's `label` in the
reader's language, carrying that label's `lang`, or else its `format` or its address; its
`format` follows in brackets where it is not the label. Each opens in a new tab.

## Opening and closing

The button is `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` the list under it. The list follows the
button, so Tab goes from the button into its links. It opens towards the side of the
window with room for it, and it closes:

- on Escape, from anywhere in it, handing focus back to the button. The Escape is taken,
  so a modal `dialog` or a floating panel of the host's round it stays open;
- on a press outside it;
- when focus moves out of it, leaving focus where it went, so the list never covers what
  is focused;
- when another of Clover's popups opens, such as a menu of the Canvas's controls: they
  open one at a time ([`useOneOpen`](/docs/composing#controls-of-your-own)).

The button says "Download", in the reader's language, with its `lang` where that differs
from the page's. Give it words of your own as its children, and mark them where they are
in a language other than the page's (WCAG 3.1.2):

```jsx
<Clover.Download>Download this issue</Clover.Download>
```

## In About

About lists the same links, under a Download heading at its own `headingLevel`, with its
groups a level below, and no button. So `Default`, whose About is in its panel, has no
Download button. `Document` has one in its toolbar, its groups a level below the title,
and `download={false}` leaves it out:

```jsx
<Viewer iiifContent={url} template={Document} download={false} />
```

For one resource's `rendering` alone, as a plain list of links, use
[`Rendering`](/docs/primitives/rendering).

## Props

| Prop           | Type                    | Description                                                                          |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `children`     | `ReactNode`             | What the button says. Default: "Download", translated.                               |
| `headingLevel` | `2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | The level of the groups' headings, one below the heading it sits under. Default `3`. |

Its other props (`id`, `className`, `style`, `aria-*`) go to the `div` round the button
and its list. It takes no `as` or `ref`.

### Styles

| Class                     | What it is                                                      |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `.clover-download`        | The part, with `data-open` and `data-align` (`start` or `end`). |
| `.clover-download-button` | The button.                                                     |
| `.clover-download-panel`  | The list under the button, at least 14rem wide.                 |
| `.clover-download-groups` | The groups, here and in About.                                  |
| `.clover-download-group`  | A group, under its heading (`.clover-download-title`).          |
| `.clover-download-list`   | A group's links (`.clover-download-link`).                      |
| `.clover-download-format` | A link's format, after its label.                               |
