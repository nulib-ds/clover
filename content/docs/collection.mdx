# Collection

Two parts for the Collection the Root's Manifest is in. `Collection` describes it and
lists its members, as the `Panel`'s Collection section does. `CollectionSelect` is a menu
of its members, to move between them. Choosing a member in either shows its Manifest in
the Root. Both read the Root, so place them inside a `Clover.Root`.

```jsx
import * as Clover from "@nulib/clover";
import "@nulib/clover/styles.css";

<Clover.Root iiifContent={collectionUrl}>
  <Clover.CollectionSelect />
  <Clover.Content />
  <Clover.Collection />
</Clover.Root>;
```

## Which Collection

Given a Collection as its `iiifContent`, the Root shows its first Manifest, or the one
`manifestId` names, and that Collection is the Root's. Choosing a member that is itself a
Collection enters it: it becomes the Root's Collection, and its first Manifest is shown.
A paged Collection's members are followed through `next`.

Given a Manifest, the Root has no Collection until a `Collection` part asks for the
first Collection the Manifest is `partOf`. That Collection is fetched the first time the
part shows, never before. Once it arrives it is the Root's, so a `CollectionSelect`
inside the same Root lists its members too. A Collection that will not load leaves the
Manifest as it was.

## `Collection`

The Collection's label, as a heading; its summary; its members, as a list; then its
`homepage` and its own address, each under a heading. Until a `partOf` Collection
arrives, it shows what the Manifest's `partOf` says of it. It renders nothing when there
is no Collection. Laid out as About is, it is the `Panel`'s Collection section, shown
whenever the Manifest was reached through a Collection or is `partOf` one.

The members are [`Items`](/docs/items) with `of="members"` and `layout="list"`: each by
its thumbnail, label and summary, the Manifest shown selected. It is a listbox with one
tab stop. The arrows, Home and End move through the members, and Enter, Space or a click
shows the one in focus, since each loads a Manifest.

Below, the White catalog from Clover's [mocks](/docs/mocks), which is `partOf`
Northwestern's Collection of bicycle catalogs. The part fetches it as it shows. It sits
under this page's `h2`, so it is given `headingLevel: 3`. Its members are Northwestern's
records, live: choosing one shows it above, in place of the mock. Here the part scrolls in
a box of its own.

`headingLevel` (default 3) is the level of its headings: the Collection's label, and the
headings over its homepage and its address. Set it one below the heading the part sits
under. In the `Panel`'s own Collection section, the panel sets it.

### Props

| Prop           | Type                    | Description                             |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `headingLevel` | `2 \| 3 \| 4 \| 5 \| 6` | The level of its headings. Default `3`. |

Its other props (`id`, `className`, `style`, `aria-*`) go to its element, a `div`, and so
does its `ref`. It carries `clover-about` and `clover-collection`; its label is
`.clover-collection-title`.

## `CollectionSelect`

A menu of the Root's Collection's members: a native `<select>`, each member an option
named by its label (or its address, with none). It is labeled, visibly, by the
Collection's label, or by "Collection", in the reader's language, for a Collection with
none. Choosing a Manifest shows it; choosing a nested Collection enters it. It renders
nothing outside a Collection, and for a Collection of one member.

```jsx
<Clover.Root iiifContent={collectionUrl}>
  <header>
    <Clover.CollectionSelect />
    <Clover.Label as="h2" />
  </header>
  <Clover.Content />
</Clover.Root>
```

Antonio Fava", nine Manifests to choose from.

Each choice loads a whole Manifest, so the keyboard only moves through the members until
the reader makes one (WCAG 3.2.2):

| Key                                                 | Action                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| The arrows, Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, a letter | Move through the members; nothing loads  |
| Enter                                               | Shows the member moved to                |
| Escape                                              | Takes the step back, to the member shown |

Leaving the menu takes the step back too, so a Manifest loaded once focus has moved on
never takes away the control focus moved to (WCAG 2.4.3). Escape is handled only when
there is a step to take back; otherwise it is left to what is round the menu, a dialog or
a panel that floats over the page, say. A choice from the menu's own list (with a
pointer, a phone's picker, or a list opened by Alt+Down or on macOS) is made at once.

The choice holds while its Manifest loads. One that fails to load stays in the menu, and
Enter asks for it again. With the Root's `manifestId` controlled, the Root only asks
(`onManifestChange`) and moves when you hand the choice back.

### Props

| Prop       | Type                   | Description                                           |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `onChange` | `(id: string) => void` | A member was chosen: its `id`, before the Root moves. |

Its other props (`id`, `className`, `style`, `aria-*`) go to its element, a `div` round
the label and the menu, and so does its `ref`. The label is
`.clover-collection-select-label` and the menu `.clover-collection-select-control`.

## From Clover 3.x

Clover 3.x's [Composing](/docs/3.x/composing) read the Manifest's `partOf[0].id` and gave
it to a `Slider`. Inside a Root, `Collection` reads `partOf` itself, fetches the
Collection when it first shows, and shows a chosen member in the Root. For the Slider's
links to each member's `homepage`, use [`Items`](/docs/items#a-collection) on its own,
with the Collection's address.
