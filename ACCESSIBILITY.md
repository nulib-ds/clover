# Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 at Level AA is this repository's prime directive: non-negotiable, for the
package and the docs site alike (`AGENTS.md`, `packages/clover/DESIGN.md` §0). This file
holds what is known to fail and the plan to clear it. Fixing any of it is always in
scope; when you do, delete its row below and its id from the plan.

## What a resource provides

Presentation 4 lets a `supplementing` annotation say what its file is for: `provides`,
an array of values from IIIF's
[registry of accessibility values](https://iiif.io/api/registry/accessibility/), and
clients "should process" it. It is the publisher's own word on which accessibility
features a resource offers, and how a viewer should offer them, so it carries weight for
WCAG here and for how annotations are written from now on (Mat, October 29, 2026). Clover
reads it wherever it offers such a feature, and never lets a file's label, name or
extension stand in for it.

| Value                                      | What it is for                                                 | Criterion                    | In Clover                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `closedCaptions`                           | What is heard: speech, and the sounds that carry meaning       | 1.2.2 Captions (Prerecorded) | A captions track (October 29, 2026); also where nothing is provided, as in P3      |
| `subtitles`, `translation`                 | What is said, in a language, for a reader who hears it         | None; its language, 3.1.2    | A subtitles track, offered beside the captions (October 29, 2026)                  |
| `transcript`                               | The words, without the sounds captions describe                | 1.2.1, 1.2.8                 | The Transcript only; never offered as captions, which it is not (October 29, 2026) |
| `audioDescription`                         | A recording's visual information, spoken                       | 1.2.3, 1.2.5                 | Not read yet: a sound to offer beside the video                                    |
| `alternativeText`, `longDescription`       | A text alternative for an image, sound or video, short or long | 1.1.1                        | Not read yet                                                                       |
| `signLanguage`                             | The recording interpreted in a sign language                   | 1.2.6 (AAA)                  | Not read yet                                                                       |
| `highContrastAudio`, `highContrastDisplay` | A version easier to hear, or to see                            | 1.4.7, 1.4.6 (AAA)           | Not read yet                                                                       |
| `braille`, `tactileGraphic`                | Forms for touch                                                | 1.1.1                        | Not read yet                                                                       |

A WebVTT file whose annotation provides only what timed text is not is offered as
captions, as before. The reader is `timedTextKind` (`packages/clover/src/components/Canvas/layout.ts`);
a feature that offers another value reads it the same way. The docs' mocks are
Presentation 3, which has no `provides`; one in Presentation 4 that offers such a file
gives it, as the Cookbook's Presentation 4 recipes do (0219, 0253).

## Audit of October 15, 2026

Fifteen auditors read the package and the site against the 55 A and AA criteria, two of
them in headless browsers (axe-core, a keyboard walk, 320px reflow and text spacing, in
light and dark). A critic sent auditors back to criteria they had passed over. Two
independent reviewers, with a third to break ties, checked each of the 214 distinct
findings against the code and the standard; 160 held. Of those, 77 were blatant and easy
and were fixed first. Ten workstreams then fixed 44 more in the package; the rest are
planned below. The package has 79 (73 fixed), the docs 81 (54 fixed).

### Plan

What remains. Items 1 to 4 are done in code and need a check the tests cannot make.

1. **Captions in picture in picture** (1.2.2). Checked on October 16, 2026 in the iOS 26 Simulator (iPhone 17 Pro, Safari, the harness with Cookbook 0074's English and Italian captions): the system player shows the chosen track; its own Subtitles menu lists both by their labels, and Italian chosen there stays chosen, in the player again and inline; back on the page, Clover draws the one copy, and nothing is doubled. Not yet seen: picture in picture, which the Simulator did not start on Home from the system player, and Clover offers no control for. Check it on an iPhone (and Chrome's on Android, which `enterpictureinpicture` also reaches): its window shows the chosen track, and the page shows one copy afterwards. Size S. (P19)

2. **The viewport in screen readers** (4.1.2). The viewport has a role description, `aria-keyshortcuts` and its keys in words, all dropped when it cannot move. Test it in NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, where browse mode still takes the arrow keys, and then settle its role (`img` or `group`, or `application` on the viewport alone). Decide whether sighted keyboard users get a visible key hint. File: `CanvasView.tsx`. Size M. (P7)

3. **Touch with a screen reader** (2.2.1). Under a finger or a pen that does not hover, the controls wait as long as a mouse's, 3 seconds by default, before fading (Mat, 2026-11-02), and focus no press brought holds them. A screen reader's activation that sends a press, not a click alone, would fade them too: check that a second activation still reaches the faded control. Test VoiceOver in iOS Safari and TalkBack in Chrome on Android: a screen reader that moves only its own cursor sends the page no event, so a faded control could still sit unseen under it. File: `useControlsVisibility.ts`. Size S. (P104)

4. **Locale review** (3.1.2). Every locale has every string, and `locales.test.ts` keeps them complete, but no native speaker has read the new ones: es, fi, fr, ja, nb (and no, its copy), nn, pt, which is European Portuguese and what Brazilian readers get too, and it, zh-Hans and zh-Hant (drafted 2026-11-05). Mat owns this, and will make the English strings more concise and consistent, then ask translators to review. It does not block the rest of this plan, and agents leave the translations alone until then. (P56)

### Decisions

On October 21, 2026, Mat chose a text accent, `--clover-color-accent-text`, over the call of October 16 against a new token, once it was shown that no one accent passes both ways on a dark page (P51). The rest all settled on October 16, 2026: the two-tone hotspot outlines and the Split grip stay, the grip now 1px wider than the bar on each side (P9, P80); and Clover speaks the page's `<html lang>` first and the browser's languages after, for its interface language and for IIIF text (P39); Mat reversed this on November 5, 2026: the visitor's language comes first, with the site's offered beside it where they differ, as `AGENTS.md`'s "i18n" says. A focused viewport shows no key hint: its keys work (2.1.1), assistive technology hears them in its description and `aria-keyshortcuts`, and each control's tip names its key; a visible hint is help beyond AA (P7). A title cut short under a thumbnail shows in full on hover and focus, over the picture, and Default's rail titles wrap (P57). Leaving CollectionSelect takes back a step not yet chosen, and it gets no Open button: nothing loads on a change (3.2.2), a load after focus moved on could remove the control focus went to (2.4.3), and Enter, Alt+↓ and the menu's own list each choose (P54).

### Decisions needed

The focus order of a Body with many annotations, walked on November 5, 2026 (Chromium, the
harness's `composed` scene over the `rudolf-hess-nuremberg` mock, its video paused at 0:14,
where seven tracked hotspots are on the picture; Default over Cookbook 0154 and 0230;
Document, Gallery and its lightbox over 0009), for Mat to decide what stays and what changes.

What the walk found, as it is today:

- **From the view onward.** Tab goes: the Canvas's controls (Chapters, Annotations, the
  placeholder's Play), the viewport (one stop, a `group` named by the Canvas's label, its
  keys in its description), each hotspot on the picture in the order its pages give them
  (page by page, then the publisher's order within a page: the dock's front row, then its
  back row), the annotations' points on the seek bar (a `toolbar`, one stop: Left and Right
  move between the points, Previous and Next step through the annotations that share one
  point, "0:12 Hermann Göring 1 / 7"), the seek bar, Play, Mute, Volume, Captions, Fill,
  Full screen, then out of the Body. Nothing is out of reading order; a tracked hotspot
  whose time is not now is `hidden` and no stop; a page hidden from the menu takes its
  hotspots out.
- **Annotations reachable two ways.** In time order from the seek bar's points, and in
  reading order on the picture; and all of them, with their times, from the Annotations
  menu, whose radios are one tab stop across both pages (the arrows walk them), each page's
  hide toggle before its rows. A chosen annotation is `aria-current`, its point reads it
  ("0:12 Wilhelm Keitel 3 / 7"), and Escape lets it go without moving focus.
- **Leaving the view.** Past the hotspots there is no way but through them: seven here, and
  the same number of stops as hotspots drawn anywhere (N27). With the placeholder showing,
  or a page hidden, they are gone.
- **Focus on a change.** Choosing from the menu (arrows, Enter, a press) never moves focus:
  the hotspot takes `aria-current` and shows its name; the menu stays open for the next.
  Hiding a page keeps focus on its checkbox. A tracked hotspot whose time passes while
  focused hands focus to the viewport. Opening a menu puts focus on the checked item; Escape
  in it returns focus to its button; Tab on into the viewport now closes it (this audit),
  while Tab or Shift+Tab among the controls leaves it open for the next Escape (DESIGN §0,
  `focus.spec.ts`).
- **The Map beside the Body** (0154, Default). The Map control opens a group over the
  viewport, after the viewport in the DOM: Close, MapLibre's canvas (a `group`, focusable),
  Zoom in, Zoom out, the credits, then the Split's handle. Opening it leaves focus on the
  Map control, so Tab goes through the viewport before reaching it. Escape in it closes it
  and returns focus to the control. Standalone (`?scene=map`) the order is the canvas, Zoom
  in, Zoom out, the credits, then the places list, its text alternative. Sensible; the Map
  itself pans by drag alone (N26).
- **The templates.** Default: the title's links, the Canvas's controls, the viewport, the
  Items strip (one stop, its Next), the Split's handle, "More information", the panel's
  sections and their links; the DOM is the visual order, the panel toggle at the top right
  of the Body but after the handle in the DOM (it is the spine's, beside the panel). With a
  Collection (0230), the header's pager comes first: its count ("Item 1 of 2" now), Next.
  Document: the thumbnails' column, the handle, "Show items", the controls, the viewport.
  Gallery: the grid (one stop); Enter opens the lightbox, where focus lands on the strip's
  current item, and Tab runs the strip's Next, the handle, "More information", the panel,
  then wraps to Back, Close, the controls and the viewport; Escape closes it and returns
  focus to the grid's cell. The lightbox's header (Back, the title, Close) comes before the
  Body in the DOM but after it in the tab cycle from where focus enters (N15's plan covers
  where it enters).

The questions:

1. **Hotspots as one stop or many** (N27). Keep every hotspot a tab stop (reading order
   is kept, but there is no way past them), or make them a roving group (one stop into the
   picture, the arrows and Home/End between them, as Items and the seek bar's points are)?
   The recommendation is the roving group, with Tab and Shift+Tab leaving at once, the keys
   said in `canvasKeysAnnotations`.
2. **Where focus goes on a choice from the menu.** Today nowhere: the picture shows the
   pick, the menu stays open. Keep that (the reader's next arrow is the next annotation), or
   send focus to the chosen hotspot on Enter and close the menu, as a listbox's choice would?
   The recommendation is to keep it, and close the menu on Enter only where the pick frames
   a region (an image), focus to the hotspot; a timed pick plays, focus staying.
3. **The Map control's group.** Move focus into it as it opens (Close first), as the caption
   does, so Tab from the control does not pass the viewport first? The recommendation is yes:
   it is a popup the control opens, and Escape already returns focus.
4. **Default's panel toggle.** It is drawn among the Body's controls (top right) while the
   panel is closed and on the spine while it is open, and in the DOM it follows the Split's
   handle. Keep it (focus follows it as it moves, `useFollowFocus`), or draw it first in the
   panel's pane? The recommendation is to keep it: its place in the DOM is its place on
   the page, beside what it opens.
5. **A way past a long Body.** Where a Body holds a recording with its transport, a long
   Items strip or many hotspots, should the viewport's description say how to leave it
   (Tab to the next part), or should the Root offer a skip to the part after the Body? The
   recommendation is neither until N27 is decided: with hotspots one stop, the Body is at
   most a dozen stops.
